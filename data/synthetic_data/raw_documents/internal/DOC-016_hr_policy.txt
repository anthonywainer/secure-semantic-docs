HR POLICY: REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES
===================================
Owner: HR | Classification: Internal | Retention: 2 years

PURPOSE
Define expectations and requirements for employees working remotely.

ELIGIBILITY
All permanent employees in roles not requiring physical presence are
eligible for remote work. Contractors follow client-specific policies.

REQUIREMENTS
- Maintain a dedicated, ergonomic workspace.
- Use VPN at all times when accessing company systems.
- Attend all scheduled team meetings during core hours (10:00–15:00 local).
- Report equipment issues to IT within 24 hours (ref: PROJ-BJC-798).

SECURITY
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN.
- Lock your screen when stepping away.
- Report any suspected security incidents to: osbornejeffery@example.net

EXPENSES
Pre-approved home-office equipment up to $277,434.87 per year is
reimbursable. Submit via the HR portal with employee ID EMP-870831.

This policy is reviewed annually.
