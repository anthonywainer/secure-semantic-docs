DATA QUALITY POLICY v2
=======================
Owner: Data Platform | Classification: Internal | Retention: 5 years

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the data quality standards that all datasets
ingested into the lakehouse must meet before promotion to Silver.

2. QUALITY DIMENSIONS
- Completeness: mandatory fields must not be null.
- Accuracy: values must match defined enumeration sets.
- Timeliness: ingestion lag must not exceed SLA thresholds.
- Consistency: referential integrity across layers must hold.

3. ENFORCEMENT
Quality checks are run automatically by the pipeline after each
Bronze write. Failing datasets are quarantined in a _rejected
partition and an alert is raised in the monitoring channel.

4. ESCALATION
Unresolved quality failures older than 24h must be escalated to
the platform lead. Reference project code PROJ-ENK-564 in tickets.

5. REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually or after any major incident.
