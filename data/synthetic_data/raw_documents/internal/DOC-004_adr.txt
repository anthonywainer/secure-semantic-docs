ARCHITECTURE DECISION RECORD: LAKEHOUSE MIGRATION
==================================================
ADR-042 | Status: Accepted | Date: 2024-03-12

CONTEXT
The legacy data warehouse could not support the volume of unstructured
documents required by the semantic search initiative. A modern lakehouse
was selected to provide flexibility and open formats.

DECISION
Migrate to an Apache Iceberg lakehouse with a Bronze / Silver / Gold
medallion architecture. Use PySpark as the compute engine.

CONSEQUENCES
Positive:
- Open table format enables time-travel and schema evolution.
- Decoupled storage and compute reduce cost.

Negative:
- Migration effort estimated at 3 sprints (project code PROJ-ARU-848).
- Team upskilling on Iceberg required.

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED
- Delta Lake: rejected due to licence constraints.
- Hudi: rejected due to limited Python ecosystem support.
