COMPLIANCE POLICY: GDPR DATA HANDLING
======================================
Owner: Legal | Classification: Internal | Retention: 7 years

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all personal data processed by the data platform.

2. PRINCIPLES
- Lawfulness: data is processed only with a valid legal basis.
- Purpose limitation: data is not used beyond the stated purpose.
- Data minimisation: only necessary data is collected.
- Storage limitation: data is deleted per the retention schedule.

3. SUBJECT RIGHTS
Individuals may request access, correction, or deletion of their data.
Requests must be fulfilled within 30 days. Contact: esanchez@example.com

4. DATA TRANSFERS
Cross-border transfers require either an adequacy decision or standard
contractual clauses approved by Legal.

5. BREACH NOTIFICATION
Breaches affecting personal data must be reported to the DPO within
72 hours of discovery. Employee ID of the reporting officer: EMP-311656

6. ENFORCEMENT
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action and regulatory fines.
