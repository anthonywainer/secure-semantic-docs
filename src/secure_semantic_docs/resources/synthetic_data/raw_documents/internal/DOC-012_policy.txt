DATA RETENTION SCHEDULE
========================
Owner: Legal | Classification: Internal | Retention: Permanent

PURPOSE
Define the minimum and maximum retention periods for all data
categories managed by the platform.

RETENTION PERIODS

| Data Category          | Minimum | Maximum | Legal Basis          |
|------------------------|---------|---------|----------------------|
| Employee records       | 7 years | 10 years| Labour law           |
| Financial transactions | 7 years | 10 years| Tax law              |
| Audit logs             | 5 years | 7 years | Compliance policy    |
| Pipeline metadata      | 2 years | 5 years | Operational need     |
| Raw documents (public) | 1 year  | 3 years | Business need        |
| Incident reports       | 3 years | 7 years | Risk management      |

DELETION PROCESS
Data past its maximum retention period is deleted in the first week of
each quarter. Deletion requests are logged with approver EMP-158692.
