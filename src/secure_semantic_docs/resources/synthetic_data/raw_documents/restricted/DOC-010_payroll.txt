PAYROLL POLICY AND EXCEPTION HANDLING
======================================
Owner: Finance | Classification: Restricted | Retention: 7 years

1. SCOPE
This policy governs payroll processing and exception handling for all
employees and contractors.

2. PROCESSING SCHEDULE
Payroll is processed on the last business day of each month.
Exceptions submitted after day 25 will be applied in the following cycle.

3. EXCEPTION TYPES
- Overtime: must be pre-approved by the department head.
- Expense reimbursements: submitted via the HR portal (ref: EMP-118384).
- Salary adjustments: require Finance Manager approval.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Payroll data is classified Restricted. Access is limited to Finance and HR.
Any unauthorised disclosure must be reported immediately to: smithchristine@example.net

5. AUDIT
All payroll transactions are logged and retained for 7 years.
Discrepancies should be escalated to the Finance Manager with amount
reference: $808,412.41
